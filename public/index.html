FAQ

Common questions.

What kind of work can Fenronik automate?

We can assess repetitive tasks such as enquiry handling, reporting, approvals, document processing, data synchronisation and operational workflows. Feasibility depends on the tools, access and rules involved.

Can you build a solution around my existing tools?

Often, yes. We assess available APIs, exports and integration options before recommending an approach. If a system cannot be connected safely or reliably, we will explain the limitation.

Can you help me obtain a domain?

Yes. We can help you search for, acquire and register a suitable domain and configure it correctly.

Can I use my existing domain?

Absolutely. We can connect an existing domain and manage its DNS settings for your new site or application.

Do you only work with businesses?

No. We can explore suitable automation and software requirements from individuals, founders and businesses. The scope and complexity determine the approach.

Can you host websites on your infrastructure?

Yes, we can host your project on infrastructure we manage, or deploy to an account you own. Tell us which you prefer.

Do you offer e-commerce services?

Yes, through Fenronik Commerce Labs: procurement and sourcing support, pricing and margin modelling, listing setup and marketplace operations for small businesses selling on Amazon, eBay, OnBuy and similar marketplaces.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes: updates, troubleshooting, performance monitoring and continued improvements.

How does the project process work?

We understand your current process, design a practical workflow, build and test the solution, then deploy and improve it over time. See the process section above.
