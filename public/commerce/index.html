Platforms

Where we work.

The same disciplined approach, adapted to how each marketplace works. Scope depends on your account and category.

Amazon

For sellers who want their account and catalogue in order and their pricing on purpose.

  • Account health check and fix list
  • Listing creation and optimisation
  • Pricing with referral fees, fulfilment fees and VAT
  • FBA and FBM preparation
  • Sales improvement plan for existing listings

Typical outputs

Health check report, optimised listing copy and data, pricing sheet, prioritised action plan.

eBay

Clean, searchable listings and prices that survive eBay's fees.

  • Listing health review
  • Listing creation with item specifics and category selection
  • Title and keyword optimisation
  • Fee-aware pricing and margins
  • Shipping and returns policy setup

Typical outputs

Reviewed or new listings, pricing sheet, policy recommendations, improvement plan.

OnBuy

Get your catalogue live and priced properly on a growing UK marketplace.

  • Listing health review
  • Product data preparation and catalogue matching
  • Listing creation and bulk uploads
  • Pricing and margin set-up
  • Sales improvement recommendations

Typical outputs

Upload-ready product data, live listings, pricing sheet, review notes.

Shopify

For sellers with their own store who want it to convert and stay profitable.

  • Store and listing review
  • Product page and collection optimisation
  • Product listings and catalogue structure
  • Pricing and margin review
  • Custom design and development through Fenronik Global when needed

Typical outputs

Reviewed or new product pages, catalogue structure, pricing sheet, improvement plan.

FAQ

Common questions.

Who is Commerce Labs for?

Small businesses and new sellers who want expert help with their marketplace accounts, listings, pricing or sourcing without hiring a full team.

Which marketplaces do you work with?

Amazon, eBay, OnBuy and Shopify. If you sell elsewhere, ask and we will tell you honestly whether we can help.

What is a health check?

A structured review of your account and listings covering content quality, compliance and pricing. You receive a prioritised list of what to fix first.

Can you set up and optimise my listings?

Yes. We can create new listings or improve existing ones, including titles, bullet points, descriptions, keywords and product data.

Will you guarantee more sales?

No. Marketplaces control their own algorithms and policies. We improve what is within your control, such as listing quality, pricing and catalogue structure, and we report clearly on the results.

Do you build online stores too?

Yes. Fenronik Global designs and develops websites and web applications, including custom stores and tools for sellers.
